## Returns (from the refund policy)
- Returns are by request. The customer contacts the help desk with their order number before sending anything back. The team replies with the return address and next steps.
- Request within 30 days of receiving the order.
- Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition with tags attached, free from stains, damage or odours, and come with proof of purchase.
- Final sale: made-to-order and custom pieces, sale items and accessories.
- Original shipping fees are not refunded. The customer pays return shipping. A 10% restocking fee may apply to returns the brand did not cause.
- Refunds go to the original payment method after the item arrives and is inspected.
- Damaged or wrong items: contact the help desk within 7 days of delivery with photos, and the team will put it right. The team reviews the order, photos and replacement availability before approving anything. Ask for the order number, the expected item, the received item and photos.
- Missing item: the team checks packing and shipment records. Ask for the order number and the missing item's name.

## Shipping (policy updated 2026-11-05)
- Ships worldwide from the studio in Bali with tracked couriers (DHL, FedEx, UPS).
- Processing: ready-stock 3–5 business days. Pre-orders 10–14 business days.
- Delivery after dispatch: United States 7–14 business days. United Kingdom and Europe 10–20 business days. Customs can add a few days.
- Shipping cost is calculated at checkout by country. It's reduced from $200 and again from $300, and free from $400 to most countries.
- Import duties and taxes are not included and are paid by the customer on delivery.
- A tracking number is emailed when the order ships, and also appears under the customer's account orders.

## Rewards
- Earn points and redeem them at checkout after signing in. 500 points = $10 off the first order.
- Tiers: 5% from $300, 10% from $700, 15% from $1,500.
- Refer a friend: they get $20 toward their first order, and the customer earns 500 points when the friend checks out.

## Checkout, codes and accounts
- Account credit only works after signing in before checkout. Gift cards and discount codes go in their own checkout field. The total updates only if the code applies.
- Never ask for a full gift-card code, a password or card details in chat.
- Email change: sign in → profile settings → replace the email → save → verify from both addresses.
- Subscriptions: sign in → Account → Subscription settings → choose the subscription → payment, delivery frequency or Cancel. Changes affect future renewals only. Cancelling doesn't cancel an order that's already placed.
